Empowering Lives through horticulture and Hospitality

We build sustainable futures through career training for underserved populations.

We are a Nonprofit Training Center for the Underserved.

We are a Boutique Nursery specializing in local plants.

We will be a Cafe serving small bites, but for now we serve great coffee and fresh beignets!

Our Mission- Plantable is a nonprofit training center dedicated to offering hands-on job experience to underserved populations seeking careers in horticulture and food service. These populations include individuals who may face barriers to employment, such as people experiencing homelessness, former foster youth, and those with disabilities.

What you will find at Plantable

Training Center

We’re building bridges to opportunity by empowering individuals facing employment barriers through hands-on horticulture and food service training.

Boutique Nursery

We’re your North State plant connection! Find stunning local plants and flowers, and get expert advice to make your garden flourish.

Gathering Spot

Bring your lunch and enjoy the serene garden each week during business hours, Wednesday- Saturday 8am-1pm, drinks and fresh beignets available. Grab some friends and savor the vibrant evenings with live music and drinks during Downtown Redding’s First Fridays. Plantable is your destination for community connection.

Event Venue

Tired of the same old venues? Picture your next event surrounded by lush greenery. Plantable has brought a truly unique event experience to Redding! 

City Beautification

Be a part of Redding’s blossoming downtown! Join us monthly, starting again in the fall, as we host Clean-Up Calaboose Creek events and plan to plant downtown gardens that will make our city shine.

EVENTS

Night @the Nursery

Night@theNursery is at Plantable every Friday through the fall, weather permitting, from 6pm-9pm we hope to see you there!

As dusk settles, the garden transforms into an enchanting oasis, alive with the soulful sounds of live music drifting through the air. You can wander at your leisure, perhaps finding a cozy spot to enjoy a crisp wine, a refreshing beer, or a cold soda or sparkling water available for purchase. There will be culinary delights to savor if you’re hungry, including Plantable’s yummy paninis. And for that perfect sweet treat warm, tasty beignets are ready to complete your delightful evening under the stars.

Calaboose Creek Clean Up

CLEAN UP THE CREEK is on Saturday, October 18th from 9am-11am at Plantable 2018 California Street, entrance to nursery is on Gold Street across from the Riverfront Playhouse.

Cleaning up a creek is good for the wildlife, the environment, the aesthetics of the city and for all the people who pass by it daily. At Plantable, we love our community and want to see its beauty shine out for all to see! Free coffee and beignets! Plantable will provide tools, water, and snacks. Volunteers must sign a waiver, bring their own gloves, and wear long sleeves, pants, and closed-toed shoes.

First Fridays

We are open 5pm-8pm for Downtown Redding’s First Friday (of each month weather permitting). Enjoy an evening in the garden, live music, beer, wine, drinks, food and Plantable’s paninis and beignets will be available for purchase.

Picnic at Plantable

Picnic@Plantable is each Wednesday through Saturday (weather permitting). Bring your lunch and enjoy the garden. Drinks, yummy paninis and fresh beignets for purchase. Nursery is open each week Wednesday – Saturday 9am-2pm.

Meet Our Team

Each member of our team brings a special heart and skill set to Plantable adding to the success of our trainees.

Meet The Rest of The Team

Kirkwood Hale

Co-Founder
Kirkwood and Donna Hale’s journey spans continents, from eight years in Scotland to two years in Rwanda, fueling their vision to create impactful spaces in their community. With over 30 years of landscape design expertise through his company Garden Authority. Kirkwood has now realized his dream with Plantable: a place where beauty cultivates positive change. Kirkwood looks forward to the impact Plantable will have in the lives of those in their training program and in the Redding community and beyond. Kirkwood’s other passion is being a good dad to his grown daughter and son.

Donna Hale

Co-Founder

Donna Hale’s career as an educator, spanning more than three decades, took a transformative turn in Rwanda. While there, she managed The African Bagel Company, a training center empowering women, a model she deeply admired. This experience, along with eight years in Scotland, led her to co-create Plantable, bringing a sustainable social enterprise vision to her community. She is responsible for overseeing the day to day operations of Plantable and helping to create events for the community. Donna is also a proud mother to son, Eden, who works alongside her and her husband at Plantable, and daughter, Lily.

Nicki Thieme

Trainer

With 19 years in the horticulture industry, Nicki Thieme has nurtured a deep passion for plant maintenance and growth, evolving from a curious young woman captivated by gardens into a skilled and certified arborist. Her career has been a blend of cultivating vibrant landscapes, managing the health of towering trees, and most of all, sharing that knowledge by teaching others about the wonders of plants. Whether she’s shaping a backyard oasis or preserving an ancient oak, she aims to blend traditional with modern, creating harmonious spaces where nature and people connect.
