Lawn Mowing

Our professional lawn mowing service is the foundation of a beautiful, healthy yard that you'll be proud to show off to neighbors and guests. Using top-quality equipment and trained technicians, we deliver consistent results with proper mowing heights tailored to your specific grass type and season. Every mowing service includes precision edging, thorough string trimming, and complete cleanup with debris removal from all hard surfaces, giving you a perfectly manicured lawn without lifting a finger.

Lawn Fertilizing

Our customized fertilization programs deliver essential nutrients exactly when your lawn needs them throughout the growing season. Each application is carefully calibrated to your specific soil conditions, grass type, and local climate to promote deep root growth and vibrant green color. With over 11 years of experience in the Quad Cities region, we understand the unique challenges local lawns face and how to address them effectively with our proven fertilization techniques that restore soil health and transform thin, patchy lawns into lush, resilient turf.

Weed Control

Take back your lawn from invasive weeds with our targeted weed control services that eliminate existing problems while preventing new outbreaks. Our treatments target dandelions, crabgrass, clover, and dozens of other common lawn weeds without harming your desirable turf grass. We employ a strategic approach with pre-emergent barriers in spring and spot treatments throughout the season, maintaining a consistent schedule that breaks the weed lifecycle and delivers noticeably cleaner, healthier turf within weeks of your first treatment.
